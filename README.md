## 🐉 **What is ΝᏆΝᎫᎪ ᏴϴᎽ?**

**ΝᏆΝᎫᎪ ᏴϴᎽ** (pronounced *"Ninja Boy"*) is a **production‑grade Telegram bot** that turns your chat into a **script‑hosting powerhouse**. Upload `.py` or `.js` files — or entire `.zip` projects — and the bot:

- ✅ Installs all dependencies (`requirements.txt` / `package.json`)  
- ✅ Runs your script in an isolated sandbox  
- ✅ Streams logs directly to Telegram  
- ✅ Manages processes (start/stop/restart/delete) via inline buttons  
- ✅ Enforces subscription tiers and mandatory channels
